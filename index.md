# 3DPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

## Summary

3DPlayer collects no personal data. It is a video player for Android TV that plays videos other apps hand it, such as Jellyfin. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or what you watch to its developer.

The only outside service it uses is Google Play, for buying the player. Google handles that purchase under its own privacy policy.

## What the app collects

The developer receives no data from the app. 3DPlayer has:

- No account or sign-in
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics sent anywhere
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files beyond the video it is asked to play

Videos play on your device. The player reads a video from the address the other app gives it, and returns your playback position to that app so it can resume later.

## Purchases

The player is bought through Google Play Billing: a monthly or yearly subscription with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details, purchase history and trial eligibility, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer never sees your payment details.

The app asks Google Play which of these you own and keeps that answer on the device, so the player still opens when you're offline. The Google Play Billing Library may send Google its own diagnostic data about the purchase flow.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet for two things only:

- **Your videos**: it reads each video, and any subtitles, from the server the other app points it to, such as your own Jellyfin server.
- **Google Play**: to show prices, make purchases and check what you own.

**Bug reports.** If you choose to make a bug report in the settings, the app shows an address and a QR code on your local network. You download the report from there with a phone or computer. The report holds the device model, Android version, display and audio capabilities, the app's settings and the app's recent log. Before the log goes into the report, the app removes web addresses (keeping only the file type, such as .mkv), file paths, and access tokens, keys and passwords. The app never sends the report anywhere. The developer only sees it if you attach it to a bug report or email it yourself. The download stops when you close the report.

## Data kept on your device

The app keeps three things on the device, in its private storage, where other apps can't read them:

- Your settings
- Google Play's last answer about what you've bought
- The app's own recent log, used only for bug reports you choose to make

To delete them, clear 3DPlayer's storage in Android's settings (Apps > 3DPlayer > Clear data) or uninstall the app. Your purchases stay with your Google account and come back when you reinstall.

## Children

3DPlayer is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and its date changed. A change that collects new data will be described here before the app version that makes it is released.
